## [EN] English

### What This Guide Is

**Win.Win AI Blind Spot Guide** is a companion guide for people and organisations that use AI output but do not want to rely on polished wording alone.

An AI output can look clear, balanced, and useful while still missing something important. The missing part may be lived context, professional instinct, local judgement, approval authority, accountability, independent verification, recipient impact, consequence awareness, operational memory, or relevant context absent from the task basis.

This guide helps reviewers recognise those blind spots and choose suitable controls before AI output is relied on.

A useful companion question is:

```text
What do people know or use implicitly that has not been put into the source packet, prompt, or review basis?
```

Unstated Context Control is not the whole answer. Use the language-folder **AI Blind Spot Control Map** to identify the currently recognised Controls, Methods, and Accountable Routes relevant to the AI Blind Spot risk.

This guide is a companion to the **Win.Win AI Use Case Guide**, which shows where blind-spot risks may appear in specific AI-assisted tasks. This guide explains those risks more deeply and shows what controls may be needed.

Unstated Context Control is the Control currently developed in greatest detail. Within that Control, this guide’s deepest practical guidance concerns identifying and safely eliciting unstated context. Related guidance covers approved-source retrieval and extraction, representability, safe conversion, human-only handling, verification, accountable or specialist review, stopping, and escalation after context is identified or surfaced.

This guide does not make AI accountable, authorised, locally situated, professionally experienced, or able to carry human consequences.

### Folder Structure

This guide is organised by language and locale. Folder names follow the convention `[language]_[market]` so the scope of each folder is always unambiguous.

Each folder contains a **complete, self-contained set of materials** — scope notes, risk references, control maps, review cards, guidance, and templates — written and contextualised for that audience. Content is intentionally duplicated across folders rather than cross-referenced. This means each folder works on its own, with no dependency on any other folder.

| Folder | Language | Designed For |
|---|---|---|
| `en_HK/` | English | Hong Kong — individuals and organisations working primarily in English |
| `tc_HK/` | Traditional Chinese / 繁體中文 | Hong Kong — Traditional Chinese readers |
| `sc_HK/` | Simplified Chinese / 简体中文 | Hong Kong — Simplified Chinese readers, primarily Mandarin-speaking with Mainland Chinese background |

> **How to choose:** Use the folder that matches the language your team reads most comfortably. All folders cover the same core guide scope, AI Blind Spot risks, Control Map, review cards, detailed Unstated Context Control guidance, templates, and Hong Kong-oriented assumptions. Open that folder’s README for the full asset index and getting-started guide.

## [TC] 繁體中文（Traditional Chinese）

### 本指南是什麼

**Win.Win 人工智能盲點指南** 是一套配套指南，供使用人工智能輸出的個人及機構使用，讓他們不會只因文字看似流暢就依賴輸出。

人工智能輸出可以看似清晰、平衡且實用，但仍可能遺漏重要事項。遺漏的部分可能是：真實處境、專業直覺、本地判斷、批准權限、問責、獨立核實、受眾影響、後果意識、營運記憶，或任務依據中缺失的相關語境。

本指南協助審核者識別這些盲點，並在依賴人工智能輸出前選擇合適的控制。

一個實用的配套問題是：

```text
人們知道或在未明說的情況下使用了哪些內容，而這些內容尚未放入來源資料包、提示詞或審核依據中？
```

未明說的語境控制並非全部答案。請使用語言資料夾內的 **人工智能盲點控制圖**，識別與該盲點風險相關、目前已獲本指南認可的控制、方法及問責路徑。

本指南是 **Win.Win 人工智能應用案例指南** 的配套指南；後者說明盲點風險可能在特定人工智能輔助任務中出現的位置。本指南則更深入解釋這些風險，並指出可能需要哪些控制。

未明說的語境控制是本指南目前發展得最詳細的控制。在此控制內，本指南最深入的實務指引聚焦於識別及安全地引出未明說的語境。識別或引出語境後，相關指引涵蓋獲批准來源的檢索及擷取、可表述性、安全轉換、僅由人處理、核實、由承擔責任的審核者或相關領域專業人士審核、停止及升級處理。

本指南不會令人工智能因此承擔責任、獲得授權、置身本地實際情境、具備專業經驗，或能夠承擔由人承受的後果。

### 本指南是什么

**Win.Win 人工智能盲点指南** 是一套配套指南，供使用人工智能输出的个人及机构使用，让他们不会只因文字看似流畅就依赖输出。

人工智能输出可以看似清晰、平衡且实用，但仍可能遗漏重要事项。遗漏的部分可能是：真实处境、专业直觉、本地判断、批准权限、问责、独立核实、受众影响、后果意识、运营记忆，或任务依据中缺失的相关语境。

本指南帮助审核者识别这些盲点，并在依赖人工智能输出前选择合适的控制。

一个实用的配套问题是：

```text
人们知道或在未明说的情况下使用了哪些内容，而这些内容尚未放入来源资料包、提示词或审核依据中？
```

未明说的语境控制并非全部答案。请使用语言文件夹内的 **人工智能盲点控制图**，识别与该盲点风险相关、当前已获本指南认可的控制、方法及问责路径。

本指南是 **Win.Win 人工智能应用案例指南** 的配套指南；后者说明盲点风险可能在特定人工智能辅助任务中出现的位置。本指南则更深入解释这些风险，并指出可能需要哪些控制。

未明说的语境控制是本指南当前发展得最详细的控制。在此控制内，本指南最深入的实务指引聚焦于识别及安全地引出未明说的语境。识别或引出语境后，相关指引涵盖获批准来源的检索及提取、可表述性、安全转换、仅由人处理、核实、由承担责任的审核者或相关领域专业人士审核、停止及升级处理。

本指南不会使人工智能因此承担责任、获得授权、置身本地实际情境、具备专业经验，或能够承担由人承受的后果。
