# AI Blind Spot Elicitation Worksheet

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Use this worksheet to **apply and record a bounded current-task unstated-context check** for a real AI-supported task.

Use it to:
- identify what may be absent, incomplete, or inadequately represented in the task basis;
- locate possible sources;
- assess representability;
- select a suitable elicitation technique when appropriate; and
- record the minimum current-task handling.

Use [[Elicitation Techniques]] for the full Safe Elicitation Method. Use [[Templates/Reviewer Standing Check]] or [[When Questions Are Not Enough]] if the reviewer may lack the standing, permission, trust, authority, competence, or safe process needed to ask or act.

The worksheet does not require context to be recorded or entered into AI and is not a general case-management, investigation, safeguarding, personnel, counselling, or sensitive-record template. Use only the minimum information and route needed for the current task.

> [!warning]
> Safety before elicitation:
> - use role-based or generalised descriptions unless a real name is necessary, permitted, current, and safe;
> - do not place private history, safeguarding details, counselling information, disciplinary detail, unverified allegations, or relationship-sensitive information in ordinary AI prompts or shared worksheets; and
> - stop and route the task if trust, authority, competence, relationship access, permission, or a safe process is missing.

## 8. Minimum current-task handling

Choose one of the seven routes:

1. add the approved minimum to the task basis, only if it is accurate, necessary, permitted, current, and safe;
2. use safe conversion: give AI only the necessary safe effect. This may be a fact or source, a constraint or wording rule, an omission or review instruction, an approval boundary or human-drafting requirement, or an escalation trigger;
3. keep the underlying context human-only and use an authorised person to guide drafting, review, approval, timing, or escalation;
4. verify it against a sufficiently independent approved source, tool, record, or suitably accountable reviewer;
5. route it to an accountable or specialist reviewer;
6. stop and escalate;
7. do not use or record it.

Optional preservation decision:

[ ] Preserve a safe representation through an approved owner, location, access boundary, and review process.

Do not preserve automatically. Preservation is not an eighth current-task route.

## 9. Reviewer standing

Before choosing **Approve** or **Correct then approve**, confirm that the reviewer has enough trust, role, context, authority, competence, accountability, access, independence, safety, and permission for this task. Use [[Templates/Reviewer Standing Check]] if standing is unclear.
