# Exception Note Template

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Use this optional template when a current task has produced a **small, bounded, safe, reliable, and reusable lesson** that should be preserved through an approved owner and location.

Examples include:
- an exception;
- a local practice;
- a prior decision;
- an operational lesson;
- a relationship-sensitive warning; or
- another safe reusable context note.

Use it only after deciding that preservation is permitted and appropriate. The note is not automatically AI-ready and is not a substitute for an approved record, policy, decision, authority, safeguarding process, specialist process, or other required source.

This template may support a separate optional governed preservation decision if preservation is appropriate under the organisation’s approved process.

It does not provide a complete framework for:
- a context repository;
- metadata;
- retrieval;
- retention or deletion;
- records architecture; or
- RAG design.

---

## 2. Before using this note

Confirm that the context is:

- accurate;
- necessary;
- permitted;
- current;
- safe;
- sufficiently reliable;
- reusable;
- appropriate to preserve;
- assigned to an accountable owner and approved location.

If any answer is unclear, do not preserve the context here.

---

## 3. One-line note

```text
When [trigger or situation] happens, check [approved context, source, person or function] before [action, output or approval].
```

## 5. Examples

Approval exception:

```text
When an invoice approval exception appears to apply, check the approved finance exception route before drafting approval wording.
```

Affected-person notification:

```text
Before sending a broad announcement about a role change, check that affected people have been told directly first.
```

Venue access or setup:

```text
Before using last year’s event checklist for this venue, check access and setup notes because lift availability affected timing.
```

---

## 6. Safety warning

Do not record sensitive, private, unsafe, disputed, unnecessary, stale, unverified, non-permitted, or overly personal context in this template.

Do not use this template to bypass an approved record, policy, decision owner, safeguarding process, privacy process, legal or regulatory process, HR process, professional review, pastoral or theological review, or other specialist route.

For HR, pastoral, safeguarding, disciplinary, counselling, complaint or investigation matters, use the organisation’s proper restricted process and escalate to an accountable owner.
