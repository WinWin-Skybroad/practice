# Reviewer Standing Check

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Use this template to decide whether a proposed reviewer is suitably placed for the required task.

Check whether the reviewer has enough:
- role;
- competence;
- authority;
- permission;
- independence;
- context; and
- accountability

to review, approve, elicit, correct, or escalate an AI-supported output.

In practical terms, **reviewer standing** concerns whether this person is suitably placed to perform the required review, not merely whether a reviewer has been assigned.

The template operationalises the Reviewer Standing Check Method and helps identify when the task should move to a more suitable Accountable Route.

It is a practical current-task check. It is not a complete framework for role design, delegation, approval governance, HR, professional credentials, or organisational authority.

## 4. Red flags

Escalate or reassign review if:

- the reviewer is careful but lacks domain knowledge;
- the reviewer knows the task but lacks authority;
- the reviewer is too close to the situation and may fill gaps from memory;
- the reviewer is too junior or too exposed to ask about sensitive context;
- the reviewer cannot safely disagree;
- a reporting relationship, seniority difference, or organisational pressure affects the answer;
- the reviewer lacks permission to handle the context;
- the task requires an approved multi-person process;
- the matter should remain human-only;
- the task involves conflict, personnel history, safeguarding, privacy, legal, financial, regulatory, pastoral, counselling, theological, professional, or other specialist matters;
- the output may affect a person’s rights, role, access, reputation, safety, or trust.
