# 3P Framework — Safe AI Use

> **Three questions. Before every AI task. Every time.**
>
> AI is a powerful drafting tool. These three questions help you use it safely — not to slow you down, but to make sure you stay in control of the output.

---

## 1. P1 · Purpose

### Why are we using AI for this task?

- **Clear purpose?** — not just "try AI and see"
- **Right task type?** — text-heavy, repetitive, or information-based
- **Right tool?** — or would a human be better?
- **Risk check** — does AI reduce risk here, or create new risk?
- **Human reviews output** — I will review it before using it
- **Named owner?** — who is responsible for this output?
- **Accelerate, not escape** — responsibility stays with you

**AI is usually good for:**
- Drafting — announcements, emails, newsletters, outlines
- Summarising — long documents, meeting notes, reports
- Brainstorming — ideas, options, structures, first approaches
- Creating first drafts — creative content that a human then edits and owns
- Translation support — first-pass drafts with bilingual human review

> [!warning] Stop if the task affects another person's health, finances, safety, or legal status:  
> These decisions need a human — not a language model.

---

## 2. P2 · Permission

### What is allowed — and what is not — with our data and tools?

- **Approved tool?** — right tool for this task and data type
- **Know the limits** — what data is NOT permitted in this tool?
- **PDPO check** — is this data permitted for AI processing? (DPP3)
- **Privacy policy checked** — does the tool allow this data type?
- **No personal data** — not pasting names, IDs, addresses, phone, health, or financial details
- **No confidential elements** — not pasting pastoral records, client records, or sensitive information

> [!note] The simple rule:  
> If you would not write it on a postcard and leave it on a public notice board — do not put it into a public AI tool.

**Never enter into a public AI tool:**
- **Personal data** — names, HKID, contact details, health, or financial records
- **Confidential data** — client, HR, or pastoral information
- **Credentials** — passwords, API keys, or login details

---

## 3. P3 · Proof

### Can we verify the output and be accountable for it?

- **Read full output** — before it reaches anyone
- **Can stand behind it?** — comfortable if challenged
- **Verify claims** — figures, names, and citations verified
- **Keep a record** — prompt and output noted for important tasks
- **Can explain it?** — able to say in plain language why this output is good enough.
- **Can we correct it?** — if something goes wrong, can we explain and fix it quickly?

> [!important] The accountability test:  
> Would I be comfortable putting my name on this output and explaining what AI helped produce?
> If yes — you have done enough. If no — check more.

> [!note] Polished is not proof. 

---

## If You Cannot Answer All Three — Pause

Do not proceed until you can answer P1, P2, and P3 clearly.

---

## Quick Reference

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **AI gives a statistic** | Search the primary source before using it |
| **AI gives a URL or citation** | Check it actually exists |
| **About to paste personal data** | Stop — use anonymous examples instead |
| **Output to an external party** | Human review required before sending |
| **HK law or compliance** | Verify on elegislation.gov.hk or with a solicitor |
| **Unsure if AI suits this task** | Ask: "What if this output is wrong?" |

---

## Hard Stops — Always Stop If:

- **People decisions** — access, eligibility, or welfare of a person
- **Data in prompt** — personal or confidential content entered
- **Professional judgment** — legal, medical, pastoral, or compliance output
- **No human reviewer** — output reaches someone without a human reading it first
- **Looks confident?** — that is not proof; always verify

---

## Not Suitable for AI — Seek a Human Instead

- **Medical decisions** — diagnosis or treatment
- **Legal advice** — compliance rulings
- **Financial decisions** — investment choices
- **Pastoral care** — counselling or spiritual direction
- **High-impact decisions** — significantly affecting another person's life

---

## HK Reminders

- **PDPO before you prompt** — personal data collected for one purpose cannot be used for AI without assessment
- **Check TC documents** — scanned Traditional Chinese documents are often not machine-readable; test before processing
- **Human review** — non-negotiable standard for every AI output before it reaches anyone

---

> *Before you use AI day-to-day — ask 3P.*
> *Before you commit to an AI project — ask 3R.*
> *If you cannot answer all six clearly, pause and think it through first.*
