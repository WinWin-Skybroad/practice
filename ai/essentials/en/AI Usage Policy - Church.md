## 1. Purpose
> [!note] Adapt this opening sentence to reflect your organisation's specific faith commitments and values.

We believe technology should serve people and honour God. AI tools offer genuine benefits for administrative efficiency, communication, and learning. Used well, they can free up time for ministry. Used carelessly, they can damage trust, compromise confidentiality, and replace human care with something that only resembles it.

This policy exists to help our staff and volunteers:

- **Use AI wisely** — within clear and agreed boundaries
- **Protect privacy** — the dignity of every person we serve
- **Preserve integrity** — of our ministry and communications
- **Keep pastoral care human** — spiritual direction and theological teaching belong to called human ministers
- **Meet legal obligations** — under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)

This policy does not prohibit the use of AI. It establishes the boundaries that allow AI to be used in a manner consistent with our values, our legal obligations, and our duty of care to the people God has entrusted to us.

## 2. Scope

- [ ] **All paid staff** of [Organisation Name]

- [ ] **Personal AI accounts** — e.g. personal ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini accounts used for any church-related task

## 3. Our Governing Principles

All AI use within this organisation is governed by the following principles:

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Human review is mandatory** | No AI output is used, published, sent, or distributed without a human reading and approving it first. Speed is never a reason to skip this step. |
| **Pastoral care remains human** | AI has no role in pastoral counselling, prayer ministry, spiritual direction, or any form of care for a person's soul. These require a human presence — always. |
| **Theological teaching remains human** | AI must not be positioned as a source of theological authority. Scripture interpretation, doctrinal guidance, and spiritual teaching belong to called and accountable human ministers. |
| **Personal data is protected** | Member, visitor, or beneficiary personal data is not entered into public AI tools under any circumstances. |
| **Truth over speed** | AI-assisted content that is sent or published represents our organisation. Accuracy matters more than efficiency. We do not publish AI output without reading it. |
| **Honesty is maintained** | We do not use AI to create deceptive content — including AI-generated voices, images, or text that misrepresents our leadership, doctrine, or the people we serve. |

## 6. Prohibited Use Cases — The Red Lines

The following uses are **not permitted** under any circumstances. These 'red lines' exist to protect our congregation, our ministry, and our organisational integrity:

- [ ] **Pastoral care via AI** — AI must never be used to provide counselling, prayer support, spiritual direction, or any form of pastoral care. These require a real human relationship.
- [ ] **Theological teaching by AI** — AI must not be presented as a source of theological authority or scriptural interpretation. Theological content generated by AI must be clearly attributed as such and reviewed by a qualified minister before any use.
- [ ] **Personal data in public AI tools** — Member names, contact details, HKID numbers, health information, financial details, prayer requests, counselling notes, or any personal information about individuals must never be entered into a public AI tool.
- [ ] **Confidential pastoral records** — Counselling records, personal disclosures, and pastoral care files must never be shared with any AI system.
- [ ] **Automated external communications** — AI must not send any communication to members, visitors, or the public without per-message human review and approval.
- [ ] **Beneficiary eligibility decisions** — AI must not make or recommend decisions about a person's access to care, financial assistance, or ministry participation.
- [ ] **Deceptive AI-generated content** — AI-generated voices, images, or videos that misrepresent our leadership or fabricate statements from real people are prohibited.
- [ ] **Unapproved tools** — AI tools not on the approved list in Section 4 must not be used for organisation-related tasks without prior written approval from the Policy Owner.

## 7. Required Safeguards for All Approved Uses

The following safeguards apply to all approved AI use:

- [ ] **Human review** — every AI output shared with or affecting congregation members, the public, or any external party must be reviewed and approved by a named responsible person before use
- [ ] **No sensitive data** — no personal data, pastoral records, HR data, or confidential documents are entered into a public AI tool
- [ ] **PDPO compliance** — where AI tools process any personal data, PDPO Data Protection Principle 3 compliance must be assessed first
- [ ] **TC readability** — where TC documents are used, they must be tested for machine-readability before AI processing
- [ ] **Source verification** — all facts, figures, citations, and claims in AI output are verified against primary sources before use
- [ ] **Theological review** — any AI-assisted content with theological, doctrinal, or pastoral implications is reviewed by a pastor or elder

## 8. Data Protection and Privacy — PDPO Compliance

All AI use must comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong. The following rules are mandatory:

| PDPO Requirement | How we comply in AI use |
|---|---|
| **DPP 1 — Collection purpose** | We only use personal data in AI tools for purposes consistent with why that data was originally collected — and only where explicitly permitted |
| **DPP 3 — Use limitation** | Personal data collected for ministry purposes (e.g., event registration) is not processed through AI tools for a different purpose (e.g., communications profiling) |
| **DPP 4 — Data security** | Personal data is not entered into public AI tools. Only anonymised or non-personal information is used in AI-assisted tasks |
| **DPP 5 — Openness** | Staff and volunteers are informed about this policy and how the organisation uses AI tools |

> [!warning] Before using AI on any task involving member or beneficiary data, ask: **Was this data collected for a purpose that includes AI processing?** If not, it must not be used. If you are unsure whether a specific use of personal data in an AI tool is permitted under PDPO — do not proceed. Consult the Policy Owner first.

## 9. Human Review and Quality Control

Human review is not optional. It is a hard requirement for all AI-assisted output.

| Output type | Required reviewer | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| External communications (emails, newsletters, social media) | Responsible ministry leader or communications team | Before sending or publishing |
| Internal notices and memos | Sending staff member or volunteer | Before sending |
| Meeting summaries and minutes | Meeting chairperson or secretary | Before distributing |
| Volunteer materials and onboarding documents | Operations manager or ministry leader | Before sharing with volunteers |
| Translated content | Bilingual staff member or qualified reviewer | Before publishing or sending |
| Theological or scripture-adjacent content | Senior pastor or designated minister | Before any use whatsoever |

The reviewer is responsible for:

- **Read fully** — not just skimming
- **Verify all facts** — names, dates, and figures
- **Correct errors** — before the content is used
- **Take responsibility** — for the final content as if they had written it themselves

## 10. Shadow AI (Using Personal Tools for Church Tasks)

Shadow AI means using personal AI subscriptions for church tasks, bypassing the organisation's approved tools.

- [ ] **Consumer terms only** — personal AI accounts are not enterprise-protected
- [ ] **No church data** — personal AI tools must not be used for tasks involving member data or confidential church information
- [ ] **Training risk** — personal accounts may use inputs for AI training
- [ ] **Raise, don't bypass** — if better tools are needed, raise with the Policy Owner; do not work around the gap using personal accounts

---

## 11. The Training Opt-Out Requirement

Many AI tools use inputs for model training by default. When using any AI tool for organisation-related tasks:

- [ ] **Check default settings** — do your approved tools allow inputs to be used for training?
- [ ] **Opt out if possible** — opt out before using the tool for any organisational content
- [ ] **Do not use if no opt-out** — if opt-out is not available, do not use that tool for any content containing organisational information

This applies especially to free or personal AI accounts used for church tasks.

## 12. Reporting and Breach Management

Staff or volunteers must report the following to the Policy Owner immediately:

- **Unreviewed output shared** — any AI output shared or acted upon before required human review
- **Personal data exposed** — any personal data accidentally entered into a public AI tool
- **Policy violation** — any AI use that may have violated this policy or applicable law
- **Uncovered situation** — any concern about AI use not covered by this policy

Reports will be treated with confidentiality and without blame where the concern is raised in good faith.

If personal data may have been accidentally exposed through an AI tool, this may trigger an obligation under the PDPO. The Policy Owner will determine whether notification to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) is required.

---

## 13. If Something Goes Wrong

If AI produces an error that has already been shared with congregation members or the public:

1. **Notify leadership** — immediately; do not try to quietly correct it
2. **Act transparently** — not attempting a quiet fix
3. **PDPO assessment** — if personal data was involved, assess breach notification obligations
4. **Correct promptly** — through the appropriate channel as quickly as possible
5. **Update the process** — review what safeguard should have caught this

## Appendix B — Quick Reference for Staff and Volunteers

**Before using AI for any task, ask:**

- **Purpose** — Is this task appropriate for AI? Is the purpose clear?
- **Permission** — Is this tool approved? Is the data safe to use?
- **Proof** — Will a human review this before it reaches anyone?

**Always avoid:**
- **Member data** — names, contacts, HKID, health, finances, prayer requests: never in any AI tool of members, visitors, or beneficiaries 
- **Pastoral AI** — counselling, prayer support, or spiritual guidance: human only
- **Unreviewed output** — publishing or sending AI output without reading it first

**When in doubt — ask [Policy Owner name/role] before proceeding.**
