## 6. Stop Conditions

This engagement pauses or ends immediately if any of the following occur:

- [ ] **Sensitive data enters scope** — personal, pastoral, HR, or financial data enters scope unexpectedly
- [ ] **Scope changed materially** — client changes the problem or deliverable from what is described in Part B
- [ ] **Out-of-scope request** — client requests implementation, compliance sign-off, or legal interpretation
- [ ] **No named reviewer** — client is unable or unwilling to name a reviewer or approver for any output
- [ ] **Guaranteed outcomes expected** — client expects guaranteed outcomes from any recommendation
- [ ] **Relationally inappropriate** — engagement becomes relationally or spiritually inappropriate for either party
- [ ] **Repeated scope breach** — client repeatedly requests work outside the agreed scope

*If the engagement stops for any of the above reasons, Win.Win@Skybroad will provide a brief written explanation and, where appropriate, suggest an alternative approach or referral. Where an engagement ends for pastoral or relational reasons, Win.Win@Skybroad will, with the client's agreement, suggest a referral to an appropriate person or organisation.*
