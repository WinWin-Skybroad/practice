# HK Safe AI Use Quick Reference

**Practical reminders for safer AI use in Hong Kong** 

> In Hong Kong, safer AI use is not only about prompts. It is also about **PDPO, human review, Traditional Chinese document quality, and honest boundaries**.

## 1. Where AI helps in Hong Kong

* draft internal announcements
* summarise typed documents
* prepare bilingual first drafts
* brainstorm public wording
* reformat rough notes

## 2. PDPO Before You Prompt [^1]

- Personal data collected for one purpose must not be reused for AI casually
- If personal data is involved, stop and assess first
- If unsure whether the data is allowed, do not proceed

*PDPO = Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. It limits how personal data may be reused. DPP3 = the specific principle that says data collected for one purpose must not be used for another without assessment.*

## 3. Use The Postcard Rule [^2]

Before entering anything into a public AI tool, ask:

**Would I be comfortable writing this on a postcard and leaving it on a public notice board?**

If no:
- do not paste it
- anonymise it
- or use a safer approved workflow

## 4. Human Review Is Non-Negotiable [^3]

- AI output is a **draft**, not truth
- Read the full output before using it
- Verify names, dates, figures, citations, and links
- Never let external content go out without human review

## 5. Check Traditional Chinese Readability

- **Native digital files** (Word, typed PDF) are generally reliable
- **Scanned or image-based documents** carry higher risk
- **Failure behavior:** AI may partially read, omit, or silently “correct” characters (especially in handwritten or low-quality scans), which can change the meaning of the text
- **Mandatory spot check:** If the source is an image, compare at least one full paragraph of the AI output against the original image before use
- **Test before scaling:** Test readability on a one-page sample before processing multiple documents
- **Use caution with low-quality images:** If the document is blurry, faint, or crowded, accuracy will drop significantly — do not rely on AI output without careful verification

## 6. Hong Kong Law And Regulation Need Official Checking

- Do not trust AI alone on HK legal or compliance matters
- Check official Hong Kong sources where relevant
- If the matter is serious, involve a qualified Hong Kong professional

> Key sources: www.pcpd.org.hk · www.elegislation.gov.hk

## 7. Personal Accounts Create Shadow AI Risk [^4]

- Personal AI subscriptions are not enterprise-protected
- Do not use personal tools for church, client, HR, or confidential work data
- Check training settings and opt out if possible
- Raise the tool gap - do not bypass it quietly

*Shadow AI means using your personal AI subscriptions (such as a personal ChatGPT or Gemini account) for church or work tasks, without the organisation knowing. This creates data risks the organisation cannot control.*

## 8. Final Safe-Use Habit

Before using AI output, ask:

- **Purpose** - why am I using AI here?
- **Permission** - am I allowed to use this data and tool?
- **Proof** - can I verify this before anyone relies on it?

If all three are not clear, pause.
