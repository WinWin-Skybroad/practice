# Red Lines Quick Reference

**These red lines exist so you can use AI confidently in lower-risk tasks without drifting into over-reliance.**

**Hard stops for AI use in churches, SMEs, nonprofits, and community organisations**

> If one of these applies, **pause first**. Do not use AI until a named human reviews the situation.

## 1. Never Put This Into A Public AI Tool

- **Personal data** - names, HKID numbers, contact details, health information, financial details [^1]
- **Confidential records** - client files, HR records, contracts, internal pricing, unpublished financials [^1]
- **Pastoral or counselling records** - prayer requests, care notes, personal disclosures [^2]
- **Credentials** - passwords, API keys, login details, tokens [^3]
- **Anything you would not put on a public notice board** [^3]

## 2. Never Let AI Do These Jobs By Itself

- **Pastoral care** - counselling, spiritual direction, prayer ministry [^3]
- **Theological authority** - doctrine, scripture interpretation, ministry guidance without review [^2]
- **People decisions** - hiring, discipline, eligibility, welfare, access, financial assistance [^1]
- **Legal / compliance sign-off** - legal documents, regulatory submissions, tax filings [^1]
- **Medical or financial judgment** - diagnosis, treatment, investment, financial commitment [^3]

## 3. Never Send Or Publish Without Human Review

- **External communications** - no AI output goes to members, clients, or the public without a human reading it first [^2]
- **Automated sending** - no autonomous external communication without review [^5]
- **High-impact content** - no output affecting reputation, rights, safety, or wellbeing without review [^4]

## 4. Never Use AI To Mislead

- **Fake authority** - do not present AI as if it were a pastor, lawyer, doctor, or qualified professional [^2]
- **Deceptive content** - no fabricated quotations, fake citations, fake images, fake voices, or fake statements [^3]
- **Hidden uncertainty** - if you cannot explain where the answer came from, do not rely on it [^6]

## 5. If Unsure, Use The Three Questions

- **Purpose** - Is AI the right tool for this task?
- **Permission** - Is this data and tool allowed?
- **Proof** - Can I verify the output before anyone relies on it?

If you cannot answer all three clearly, stop and ask first.
