# AI Safety Checklist — Personal

> [!info] Use case record:  
>  
> **Task:** (e.g. church newsletter drafting, meeting note summarisation)  
> **Date:**  
> **AI tool used:**  
> **Filled in by:**  

> [!quote] Why this matters  
> AI tools are powerful — and easy to misuse by accident.  
>  
> This checklist helps you protect yourself, your data, and the people you serve.  
> Use it every time you try a new AI tool or task.

> Treat AI output as a draft engine, not a truth engine.  
> Every output needs a human reader before it reaches anyone or anything that matters.

**Who this is for:** Anyone using AI tools personally — for work, study, ministry support, volunteering, or side projects.

**Important:** "Safer AI use" means reducing common risks. It does **not** mean guaranteed safety, accuracy, or compliance.

---

## 1. Before You Start (3P Check)

### P1 · Purpose — Is this the right task for AI?

- [ ] **Clear purpose?** — What exactly will AI produce, for whom, and what will you still decide yourself?
- [ ] **Right task type?** — text-heavy, repetitive, or information-based; not a high-stakes judgment call
- [ ] **Right tool?** — or would a human be better?
- [ ] **Risk check** — does AI reduce risk here, or create new risk?
- [ ] **Human reviews output** — I will review it before using it
- [ ] **Named owner?** — who is responsible for this output?
- [ ] **Accelerate, not escape** — using AI to speed up drafting, not to remove my responsibility
- [ ] **No decisions about people** — not affecting health, finances, safety, or legal status

> [!warning] Stop if you are about to let AI make a decision about another person:  
> Medical, financial, legal, pastoral, and welfare decisions need a human being — not a language model.

---

### P2 · Permission — What am I allowed to put into this tool?

- [ ] **Approved tool?** — right tool for this task and data type
- [ ] **Know the limits** — what data is NOT permitted in this tool?
- [ ] **PDPO check** — is this data permitted for AI processing? Hong Kong's privacy law (PDPO) says personal data collected for one purpose should not be reused for another without assessment. When in doubt, leave personal data out.
- [ ] **Privacy policy checked** — does the tool allow this type of data?
- [ ] **No personal data** — not pasting names, IDs, addresses, phone, health, or financial details
- [ ] **No confidential elements** — not pasting pastoral, client records or sensitive information
- [ ] **No credentials** — not pasting passwords, API (Application Programming Interface) keys, or login details
- [ ] **Minimum data** — reduced to only what is necessary for this task
- [ ] **Anonymised where possible** — names and identifiers removed
- [ ] **Training opt-out** — checked privacy settings; know what data is stored or used for training

> [!note] The simple rule:  
> If you would not write it on a postcard and leave it on a public notice board, do not put it into a public AI tool.

---

### P3 · Proof — Can I verify and be accountable for what AI produces?

- [ ] **Read full output** — before it reaches anyone
- [ ] **Can stand behind it?** — comfortable if challenged
- [ ] **Verify claims** — statistics, dates, names, citations verified
- [ ] **Keep a record** — prompt and output noted for important tasks
- [ ] **Can explain it?** — able to say in plain language why this output is good enough
- [ ] **Can we correct it?** — if something goes wrong, can we explain and fix it quickly?
- [ ] **Expect errors** — AI can hallucinate confidently; I know this.

> [!note] Polished is not proof.

> [!important] The accountability test:  
> Would I be comfortable putting my name on this output and explaining what AI helped produce?  
> If yes — you have done enough. If no — check more.

## 2. After AI Responds: Hallucination Warning Signs

Watch for these and verify before trusting any of them:

- [ ] **Statistic, no source** — specific figure with no named origin
- [ ] **Citation exists?** — paper, case, or reference: check it actually exists
- [ ] **URL works?** — check it leads where it claims
- [ ] **Recent event?** — AI training has a cutoff date
- [ ] **HK law stated?** — HK-specific rules are frequently wrong in AI
- [ ] **100% confident tone** — no uncertainty or caveats: a warning sign
- [ ] **Polished, no evidence** — authoritative-sounding but nothing specific to back it up

> [!warning] Red Flags — Stop and Reconsider:  
> Stop and slow down if:
> - **Affects someone's rights** — eligibility, reputation, finances, or wellbeing
> - **Professional judgment needed** — legal, medical, financial, HR, pastoral, or compliance
> - **Unintended data included** — personal or confidential content in the output
> - **Pressured to trust it** — looks polished and confident: verify anyway
> - **Cannot explain it** — cannot explain the source, limits, or reliability
> - **No further review** — someone else will rely on this without any check

---

### 2.1 The 3-Minute Fact-Check Habit

When you spot a warning sign above — or before sharing anything important — take three minutes:

- **Doubt question (30s)** — Ask AI: "What might you be wrong about?"
- **Search one claim (60s)** — the most specific claim in a search engine or official source
- **Ask a human (60s)** — if it affects someone else, verify before acting

---

## 3. Protecting Your Accounts and Devices

> These are one-time setup checks — not required for every AI task. Review when you first use a new tool or every few months.

- [ ] **Unique password** — different from banking or work accounts
- [ ] **Two-factor authentication** — enabled on AI tools used regularly
- [ ] **Not logged in** — do not stay logged in on shared or public computers
- [ ] **Settings reviewed** — privacy settings checked; know what data is stored
- [ ] **Training opt-out** — opted out of AI training if I do not want that

---

## 4. Before I Share or Publish Anything

- [ ] **Full output read** — not just the beginning
- [ ] **Own voice** — edited to reflect my own voice; errors corrected
- [ ] **Tone and impact** — checked for accuracy and effect on others
- [ ] **Removed misleading** — anything overconfident, embarrassing, or harmful removed
- [ ] **Accountable** — comfortable if I had written this myself
- [ ] **Disclosure considered** — if AI materially helped, considered whether to note that

---

## 5. Quick Reference

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **AI gives a statistic** | Search the original source before using it |
| **AI gives a URL** | Check the link actually exists and works |
| **AI mentions a real person** | Verify from an official source |
| **About to paste personal data** | Stop — use anonymous examples instead |
| **Sending output externally** | Read fully; edit; human signs off |
| **HK law or regulation** | Verify on elegislation.gov.hk or with a solicitor |
| **Unsure if AI is right** | Ask: "What if this output is wrong and I acted on it?" |

---

## 6. Not Suitable for AI — Always Use a Human

- **Medical decisions** — diagnosis or treatment
- **Legal advice** — contracts or compliance rulings
- **Financial decisions** — investment choices
- **Pastoral care** — counselling or spiritual direction
- **High-impact decisions** — significantly affecting another person's life
- **"AI said it"** — any task where this would not be an acceptable explanation

---

## 7. High-Risk Categories — Extra Caution Always

| Category | Risk | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Medical / health** | Wrong dosages, diagnoses, or treatment | Verify with a doctor or pharmacist |
| **Legal / regulatory** | HK-specific law frequently wrong in AI | Verify on gov.hk, elegislation.gov.hk, or solicitor |
| **Financial / investment** | May be outdated or inapplicable to HK | Verify with a licensed advisor or SFC/HKMA source |
| **Scripture / theology** | Plausible but doctrinally wrong content | Verify in the Bible directly; consult your pastor |
| **Named individuals** | AI may invent biographical details | Verify from the person's official profile or contact |
| **Recent events** | AI training has a cutoff date | Search a live news source |
| **Church communications** | May affect relationships or reputation | Human pastoral review before sending |

---

## 8. One-Minute Final Check

Before you use, send, or publish anything AI helped create:

- **Purpose** — Was AI the right tool for this task?
- **Permission** — Did I keep sensitive data out of the prompt?
- **Proof** — Have I read this, checked the key claims, and can I stand behind it?

If all three are yes — you have done the minimum responsible thing.
