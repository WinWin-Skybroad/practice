# Nonprofit Quick Start

You do not need to open every file before beginning. Pick the task closest to what you need today, open that page, and use the **How To Review This Output** section before approving any AI output.

These cards help you enter this guide quickly.

---

## 1. Read This First

Before using this quick start page, read [[Start Here]] first.

This quick start page helps you find the use cases that are most relevant to your organisation type. It is not a replacement for this guide’s main orientation.

[[Start Here]] explains how to use this guide, how to choose a use case, how to read each use-case page, and why every AI output still needs human review before it is sent, shared, approved, published, or acted on.

After reading [[Start Here]], return to this page and choose one practical task to begin with.

---

## 2. Five Tasks To Start With

### 2.1 Draft Grant Applications

Funding proposals, impact narratives, and application drafts.

→ Open: [[Use Cases/Draft Grant Applications]]

**First check:** Has every metric, eligibility claim, funder rule, and required section in this application been verified against the funder’s current published guidelines and the organisation’s own programme data — not drawn from AI’s general knowledge?

---

### 2.2 Draft Communications

Donor, beneficiary, volunteer, partner, and public communications.

→ Open: [[Use Cases/Draft Communications]]

**First check:** Has a responsible person read this draft as if receiving it for the first time — checking every date, name, figure, and implied commitment — not just the overall tone?

---

### 2.3 Summarise Meeting Notes

Board, committee, and programme meeting summaries.

→ Open: [[Use Cases/Summarise Meeting Notes]]

**First check:** Has someone who attended this meeting compared every decision, action item, and action owner in this summary against the original notes — with the original document open, not from memory?

---

### 2.4 Draft WhatsApp Broadcasts

Volunteer coordination, event updates, and service-user reminders.

→ Open: [[Use Cases/Draft WhatsApp Broadcasts]]

**First check:** Does this message contain any personal information, implied commitment, wrong date, or statement that the person responsible for sending it has explicitly verified and approved — reading it as a recipient, not as the author?

---

### 2.5 Draft Volunteer Roles

Volunteer descriptions, onboarding notes, and responsibility boundaries.

→ Open: [[Use Cases/Draft Volunteer Roles]]

**First check:** Has the responsible owner confirmed that the duties, hours, supervision, training, payment or expenses, authority, safeguarding, and status match the actual arrangement — with qualified HR or legal review where any obligation or boundary is unclear?

---

## 3. One Rule

Read and check the AI output before sending, sharing, approving, or acting on it. AI may draft, summarise, translate, or structure; it must not become the final approver.
