# Source Packet Before Prompting

## 1. Purpose and Scope

A prompt is not the real starting point. The source packet is.

Before asking AI to draft, summarise, translate, research, or structure anything, gather the material and constraints the AI should use. A strong prompt cannot repair missing, outdated, or unauthorised source material.

A complete source packet can make later review more efficient by giving the reviewer a clear source, purpose, audience, authority boundary, and set of known uncertainties. It does not prove the output or reduce the review standard.

---

## 2. Minimum Source Packet

Before prompting, gather:

- current approved source version
- source date, file name, link, folder path, or version label
- intended audience
- purpose and intended use
- information that must not be included
- known constraints, exclusions, assumptions, and unresolved points
- relevant authority boundary: what the output may say, decide, promise, or recommend
- reviewer and review method
- expected output count or batch limit, and whether the named reviewer has capacity to check each output before use
- escalation route if the output raises sensitive or specialist issues

---

## 3. Source Packet Questions

Ask before prompting:

1. Is this the latest approved source?
2. Who is the output for?
3. What must the AI not include or infer?
4. What must be copied exactly from the source?
5. What facts, figures, dates, names, and commitments need source checking later?
6. Who is authorised to approve the final output?
7. Can a later reviewer find the exact source material used?
8. What would require escalation instead of ordinary review?
9. How many outputs can the named reviewer realistically check before use?

## 4. AI Blind Spot Check

The **Win.Win AI Blind Spot Guide** is the broader companion guide for deeper Blind Spot recognition and control selection. The current detailed focus of the **Win.Win AI Blind Spot Guide** is identifying and eliciting unstated context.

If the task may depend on relevant context that is absent from, incomplete in, or not adequately represented in the task basis, use the companion’s **AI Blind Spot Control Map** to choose the control family. The context may be known by people, available in current approved records, held in both, or of unclear source. Use **Organisational Context Handling** for wider current-task source and handling orientation, and **Unstated Context Control** for the detailed task-basis gap method.

The AI can help surface likely assumptions and missing-information categories, but it cannot prove that all missing context has been found. The companion does not replace independent verification, sign-off from the person with the right authority, review by the person responsible for the decision, checking with the person who owns the source, escalation, or source discipline after a challenge when those controls are required.

---

## 5. Practical Rule

Do not improve the prompt first when the source packet is incomplete.

Fix the source packet, set the requested output within real review capacity, then prompt.
